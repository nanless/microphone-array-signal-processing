Changes made for this tutorial (2026-09-22):
- Extracted samples [0, 160000), at 16000 Hz, from each of channels 1 through 16.
- Repackaged those unmodified PCM samples into one 16-channel WAV file.
- Exported channel 1 as a separate mono WAV.
- Created zero-delay equal-weight averages of channels 1-2 and channels 1-16,
  with nearest-even PCM16 quantization; no dither, no calibration, no DC
  removal, no time shifts, and no per-file normalization. Common gain is 1.
